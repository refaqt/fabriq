Thank you for helping. The rules for all Refaqt repositories are in the
[Refaqt contributor manual](https://github.com/refaqt/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md).
Read it first. In short:

- On your first pull request, you accept the
  [Refaqt Contributor Licence Agreement](https://github.com/refaqt/.github/blob/main/CLA.md)
  by posting one sentence as a comment. The check "Contributor agreement" tells you how.
- You keep your copyright. Refaqt gets a licence to use and license your work.
- Mark anything that you did not make yourself, and keep its own licence.
- New text files that we made start with an `SPDX-FileCopyrightText` and an
  `SPDX-License-Identifier` line.
